My CV

WELCOME!!

Hi, I'm Yi Qiu (邱弋 qiū yì), welcome to my website!

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Physics at Penn State, working with Prof. David Radice on computational astrophysics. I use numerical relativity simulations to study neutron star mergers, with a focus on neutrino transport and neutrino flavor conversions, and how they shape the merger ejecta, nucleosynthesis, and multi-messenger signals.

Before Penn State, I was a research assistant in astronomy at Nanjing University, and I received my B.S. in Applied Physics from Dalian University of Technology. I'm originally from Fuzhou, China.
